Cognitive Levels Guide (Bloom's Taxonomy)

All exam questions are categorized into three cognitive levels as per AICTE and NBA outcome-based education standards.

LOCQ (Lower Order)
Remember & Understand: Recall facts, fundamental definitions, basic notations, formulas, and conceptual explanations (e.g. Big-O definition, tree traversals, protocol layers).
IOCQ (Intermediate Order)
Apply & Analyze: Use concepts to solve numerical problems, trace algorithms, construct trees, calculate subnet masks, and differentiate algorithms.
HOCQ (Higher Order)
Evaluate & Create: Design system architectures, prove mathematical lemmas, evaluate security protocols, optimize dynamic programming solutions, and trade-off analysis.
💡 Revision Tip: Score maximum marks by first locking in all LOCQ questions (Group A MCQs and basic definitions), followed by methodical steps for IOCQ numericals, and clear justifications for HOCQ proofs.
